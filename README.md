3rd educationnal project
Infinite Runner


Infinite Runner where you need to avoid the obstacles that are on your way, as well as the drinks.



Controls:

Lateral Movement:
Keyboard: Left/right arrow or AD
Gamepad: Left stick or D-Pad
Jump
Keyboard: Spacebar or Up arrow or W
Gamepad: South button
Slide
Keyboard: Left shift or Down arrow or S
Gamepad: East button
